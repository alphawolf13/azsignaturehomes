Arcadia

How I work

A search filter cannot
tell you what a
street is worth.

Two houses the same size and the same year, ten minutes apart, can sit a third apart on price for reasons no portal will ever show you. Here is what happens once you hand me the search.

01

The comps come first, and you see all of them.

Three sales that justify the number, two that argue against it, and a straight answer on which set I believe. A price with nothing behind it is a guess in a nice font.

02

I walk the block, not just the house.

Which side of the street holds value, where the wash runs, what the alley looks like on a Tuesday, whose roofline you will be staring at, and whether the school boundary you are paying for actually covers that address.

03

Offers built on evidence.

Terms, timing and how the listing agent has actually been behaving all get read before we write. The strongest offer is rarely the highest one, and I will tell you when walking away is the win.

04

You keep the network after closing.

Contractors, inspectors, lenders, and the street-level read you only get from someone out in these neighborhoods every week. That stays yours whether or not you ever buy again.
