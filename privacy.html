Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you give Michael Karabatsos PLLC through azsignaturehomes.com. It is written to describe what this website actually does, not to cover every hypothetical. If something here is unclear, call 480.647.0282 and ask.

What this site collects

Nothing at all unless you type it in. There is no account, no login and no tracking pixel on this site. When you submit the consultation form, we receive the name, email address and phone number you enter, along with whether you are buying or selling, the area you named, any message you wrote, and the page you submitted from. When you request the buyer or seller guide, we receive your first name and email address.

Our web host keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses and browser information, for security and reliability.

What we do with it

We use it to answer you. Michael reaches out personally about the buying, selling or market question you raised, and sends the guide you asked for. That is the whole purpose.

Calls, texts and email

By checking the consent box you agree that Michael Karabatsos PLLC may contact you by phone call, text message and email about real estate services. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, reply HELP for help, or use the unsubscribe link in any email. You can also just ask, and we will stop.

Who else sees it

Your submission travels over an encrypted connection to an automation service that files it in Michael's client relationship manager. Specifically:

  • Make, which passes form submissions through to the CRM
  • Follow Up Boss, the client relationship manager where Michael works your inquiry
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and keeps the server logs described above
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces on this page and therefore sees your IP address and browser

Each of these is a service provider handling data on Michael's behalf. We may also share information where the law requires it, or with his employing broker where a transaction requires it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent or trade it. We do not hand your contact details to lenders, inspectors, contractors or any other vendor unless you ask us to introduce you.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics and stores nothing in your browser. If that changes, this page changes with it.

Property search

The home search, property valuation and listing pages on this site open search.azsignaturehomes.com, which runs on a separate platform operated by Lofty. Anything you enter there is governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms, not this one. Listing data comes from the Arizona Regional Multiple Listing Service and is provided for consumers' personal, non-commercial use.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email or call using the details below and we will take care of it. Arizona has no separate consumer privacy statute that grants additional rights here, but we honor these requests regardless of where you live.

Children

This site is meant for adults buying and selling real estate. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Fair housing

Michael Karabatsos PLLC conducts business in accordance with federal, state and local fair housing law. All homes are offered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status or national origin.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted here with a new date at the top. Material changes will be noted plainly rather than buried.
