How it works

Save your spot now, show up on the 21st, and the photos come to you.

  1. 01

    Pick your hour

    When you save your spot, choose 10, 11 or 12 o'clock. Spreading families across the morning keeps the line short.

  2. 02

    Meet us at the ramada

    The holiday backdrop is set and waiting at our ramada in Papago Park. The exact spot is pinned on your ticket below. Jasmin of Jasmin's Lens Photography, an Arizona portrait photographer, takes the shots. Bring the whole crew. Matching sweaters optional.

  3. 03

    Get your photos

    After the event, we email you a link to your photos. Download them, print them, put them on the card. Then post your favorite and tag Michael and Jorge to enter the raffle.
